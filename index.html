FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services.

We offer a comprehensive range of dental services including preventive care (scaling, cleaning, check-ups), restorative treatments (fillings, root canal, crowns), orthodontic care (teeth straightening), prosthetic solutions (dentures, fixed teeth), oral surgical procedures and dental X-rays.
Yes, root canal treatment (RCT) is one of our core dental services. It is performed by our qualified dental specialist to save damaged or infected teeth, relieve pain and restore tooth function.
Yes, dental X-ray facilities are available at the hospital to aid in accurate diagnosis of dental conditions, including detecting cavities, impacted teeth, cysts and jaw issues.
Yes, we provide both complete and partial dentures as well as fixed tooth replacement options. Our dental specialist will recommend the most suitable solution based on your individual dental condition and needs.
Yes, we offer orthodontic treatment for teeth alignment using fixed dental wiring. A consultation with our dental specialist will determine the appropriate treatment approach for your case.
Acupuncture is a practice from traditional Chinese medicine that involves inserting thin needles at specific points on the body. It is used as a supportive approach for pain management, neurological conditions and various other health concerns.
Acupuncture involves stimulating specific points on the body using thin sterile needles. The approach is based on traditional principles of energy flow and is used as a complementary approach alongside conventional medical treatment.
Each session begins with a brief assessment. Thin, sterile needles are then placed at specific points based on your condition. Sessions typically last 20-40 minutes. The process is generally well-tolerated by most patients.
The number of sessions depends on the individual condition, its severity and duration. Your practitioner will develop a personalized treatment plan and discuss the recommended course of sessions during your initial consultation.
Treatment suitability varies by individual. A thorough consultation and assessment are conducted before beginning any treatment. Certain medical conditions may require special consideration. Please consult our specialist for a proper evaluation.
We provide care for a wide range of skin conditions including acne, skin allergies, psoriasis, eczema, itching, rashes, fungal infections, ringworm, facial pigmentation, vitiligo (white patches), hair fall, baldness and nail problems.
Yes, acne and pimple treatment is one of our core dermatology services. Our specialist evaluates each case individually to determine the most appropriate treatment approach based on the type and severity of acne.
Yes, we provide treatment for both psoriasis and eczema. These are chronic skin conditions that require careful, ongoing management. Our specialist develops personalized treatment plans to help manage symptoms and improve quality of life.
Yes, allergy care is a key area of our practice. We evaluate and treat various types of skin allergies to identify triggers and provide appropriate treatment for symptom relief and management.
Yes, we address hair fall and baldness concerns. Our specialist evaluates the underlying causes and recommends appropriate treatment approaches. Results vary based on individual conditions and causes.
Yes, we provide care for facial pigmentation and vitiligo (white patches). These conditions are managed through personalized treatment plans developed after thorough assessment of each patient's condition.
